Your records. Your privacy.

When you press Reveal, this site submits your chosen boss, challenge settings, healing and resurrection counts to the server for community statistics.

The application stores a pseudonymous key derived from your IP address and browser user agent, along with the record and submission time. It does not store the raw IP address in the submission database. The hosting provider may keep separate access logs.

The key is used to limit submission frequency and detect repeated or unusual records. It does not reliably identify a person or device. Flagged records may be excluded from statistics; no account is banned automatically.

Submission records are retained for up to 90 days by default. Scheduled cleanup is required to enforce this while the site is idle. Hosting backups may follow a separate retention schedule.

Shared links contain your entered run values. Anyone receiving a link can read and change them. Records are self-reported, not verified game telemetry.

No live advertising or analytics trackers are included in this release. Contact details and hosting-specific policies must be supplied by the operator before a public launch.
