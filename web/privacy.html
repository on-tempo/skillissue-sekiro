Your records. Your privacy.

Reveal calculates your grade in your browser and sends the boss, score and challenge settings to the API for a read-only community comparison. It does not add a submission.

Contribute this clear explicitly saves your boss, challenge settings, healing and resurrection counts, score and submission time for community statistics.

API requests use a signed, random anonymous visitor cookie lasting up to 30 days. The submission database stores a pseudonymous key derived from that identifier, not your raw IP or user agent. Earlier records may retain keys derived from IP and user-agent data until they expire. The hosting provider may keep separate access logs.

The cookie limits contributions and detects repeats. It does not verify a person or device: clearing cookies or changing browsers creates another identity. Rankings compare submitted records, not unique players.

Exact repeats within 24 hours are not stored again. Anomaly detection produces review candidates without automatically excluding new records. Historical or manually excluded records may remain excluded.

Records older than 90 days are omitted from rankings by default. Scheduled cleanup deletes expired rows; hosting backups follow their separately configured retention schedule.

Shared links contain editable run inputs. Records are self-reported, not verified game telemetry. No live advertising or analytics trackers are included.

For site issues, use the project issue tracker. Do not post credentials or personal information publicly.
