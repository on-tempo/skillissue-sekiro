Your score is 100 − healing uses × 5 − resurrections × 15, with a floor of 0. S: 95+, A: 80–94, B: 60–79, C: below 60.

These are provisional rules for this prototype, not a validated measure of skill or a global ranking. Boss difficulty, Charmless, and Demon Bell do not add to this score.

Rankings compare you only with records submitted to this site, within the same boss and the same Charmless / Demon Bell setting. They are self-reported, and suspicious submissions may be excluded automatically.

Reflection of Strength adjusts Attack Power to a set value. Other stats and items are not normalized here, so equal scores do not imply equal skill. Official rematch guide ↗
